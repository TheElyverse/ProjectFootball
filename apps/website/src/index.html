Zero-Busywork Management

Match engine

Your ideas, visible on the pitch.

Every player has his own view of the game. He scans, remembers what he saw, weighs the options he can actually see — and then has to execute. A great playmaker finds the pass behind him; a nervous one only sees the ball at his feet.

  • Individual field of view, scanning and memory
  • Decisions separated from technical execution
  • Deterministic: same match, same seed, same result

Scouting & development

Players worth discovering.

Scouting gives you an imperfect picture: estimates and confidence, not all-knowing numbers. There is no single potential rating. Talent needs opportunity, and development depends on coaching, playing time, challenge and health.

Early bloomers, late bloomers, plateaus and breakthroughs — every generated player has his own story.

Analytics & explanations

Results you can learn from.

The engine knows what each player saw, which options he weighed and how well he executed. So your analysts don't just show statistics — they explain causes: where a passing move broke down, why a press failed, and what your team could do differently next time.

Living football world

A world that keeps moving.

The world is not a backdrop. Clubs chase their own goals and react to results — with or without you.

01

Club identity

Youth or stars, possession or pressing — shaped by coaches, academies and success, and able to drift.

02

Dynamic economy

Leagues gain and lose prestige; transfer markets react to scarcity, contracts and finances.

People & relationships

The core loop

Skip the days that don't matter.

Time advances to the next event that needs you. Critical issues interrupt automatically — the game never becomes a "continue" button simulator.

  1. 01

    Analyze

    Your action queue shows only what needs a decision.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Adjust tactics, training, squad or transfers.

  3. 03

    Delegate

    Do it yourself, or hand it to staff with a policy.

  4. 04

    Watch

    Follow the match and react when it counts.

  5. 05

    Adapt

    Understand causes, development and what's next.

What you won't find

No filler.
No cheating.

What we leave out matters as much as what we build. Same systems for you and the AI clubs — no shortcuts, no hidden hands.

  • ✕ Mandatory press conferences every week
  • ✕ Hidden momentum deciding matches
  • ✕ AI that cheats outside the rules you play by
  • ✕ Daily micromanagement of training and meetings
  • ✕ A social feed full of meaningless news
  • ✕ Sped-up physics — shorter matches, never faster players

Try the sandbox

Open source.
Open development.

The sandbox runs headlessly from the command line. Run a passing scenario, record it, and play it back tick for tick — then watch it in the browser, down to what each player sees and why he passed where he did.

Get started Read the design doc
sim-cli 
# run a passing scenario and record it
$ sim-cli --scenario pass-chain --seed 7 \
    --ticks 600 --replay-out pass-chain.json \
    --frames-out frames.json

# play it back and verify, tick for tick
$ sim-cli --play pass-chain.json
replay verified ✓
